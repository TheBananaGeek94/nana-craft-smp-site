How to join

Nana Craft SMP is invite-only, to keep the community safe and the vibes right. Everything you need to get in and get playing is in our Discord.

1

Request an invite

Join our Discord and ask for an invite. An admin will get you set up.

2

Get the modpack

The modpack and setup instructions are waiting for you in Discord. It runs on Minecraft Java 1.21.1 with NeoForge.

3

Jump in

Launch the pack and connect with the server address, also shared in Discord.
