BEFORE YOU JOIN

Rules & FAQ

For ages 14+ and adults. Please read and follow these rules to help keep the community friendly and enjoyable for everyone.

Community rules

  1. 1

    Be respectful

    Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.

  2. 2

    No hacks or cheats

    No hacked clients, cheats, x-ray, exploits, macros, or any unfair advantage. If you're unsure whether something is allowed, ask a staff member first.

  3. 3

    No spamming

    Do not spam messages, emojis, mentions, reactions, or commands.

  4. 4

    Keep content appropriate

    No NSFW, graphic, disturbing, or inappropriate content. Keep the server safe for everyone.

  5. 5

    No discrimination

    Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or attacks against someone's identity will not be tolerated.

  6. 6

    Use the correct channels

    Keep conversations in their proper channels. Check channel descriptions before posting.

  7. 7

    No advertising without permission

    No advertising other servers, websites, products, or services without staff approval.

  8. 8

    Respect staff decisions

    Staff are here to help keep the server organized. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it respectfully through the ticket system.

  9. 9

    No excessive trolling or drama

    Jokes are fine, but do not ruin other people's experiences or start unnecessary arguments.

  10. 10

    Protect your privacy

    Do not share personal information about yourself or others, including addresses, passwords, or private details.

  11. 11

    Follow Discord's Terms of Service

    You must follow Discord's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service at all times.

  12. 12

    Have fun

    Enjoy the community, meet new people, and help make this a welcoming place for everyone.

Breaking rules may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans depending on the severity of the situation.

Common questions

How do I get an invite?

Nana Craft SMP is invite-only. Join our Discord and request an invite, and an admin will get you set up.

What do I need to play?

Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1 and our NeoForge modpack. The modpack and setup instructions are in Discord.

Where do I find the server address?

It's shared in our Discord, along with everything else you need to get playing.

Is there voice chat?

Yes. The modpack includes Simple Voice Chat.
